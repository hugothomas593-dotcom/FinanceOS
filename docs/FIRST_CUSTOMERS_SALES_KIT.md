# Avenor — first-founder sales kit

Audience: European B2B SaaS founders, Pre-seed to Series A, typically 5–50 employees, without a full-time CFO.

## Positioning

**One-line promise:** Know what your startup can afford before making your next big decision.

**What we sell:** A founder-facing finance decision workspace that turns a saved cash model, operating metrics and imported cash movements into tested decisions: hiring, pricing, spend, revenue downside and fundraising.

**What we do not sell:** A replacement for an accountant, payroll provider, bank, ERP or responsible finance leader. Forecasts are simplified and depend on founder-supplied assumptions.

## First target list

Start with 30 named founders:
- 10 warm ESCP, alumni, former colleague and friend-of-friend introductions.
- 10 French/Benelux B2B SaaS founders from local startup communities.
- 10 European Seed-stage founders reached through thoughtful LinkedIn outreach.

Only contact founders whose business model and stage match the initial customer profile. Do not buy a bulk email list.

## Follow-up after 4–5 business days

## 20-minute founder conversation

### Minutes 0–5: understand their current process
Ask:
1. How do you currently track cash and runway?
2. What spreadsheet or finance tool do you open before deciding to hire?
3. When did you last change a decision because of cash constraints?
4. Who updates the model, and how often?
5. Which numbers do you not fully trust?

### Minutes 5–12: show the product
1. Start with the public demo at `/demo`; explain that Northstar is synthetic data.
2. Select "Hire two engineers" and show the month-18 cash impact.
3. Switch to "Revenue falls 20%" and discuss the difference.
4. Show the pricing and fundraising scenarios.
5. Be explicit that the public demo is a deterministic scenario explorer, not the live Copilot.
6. In a signed-in Preview session, if the live Gateway is verified, show the saved company context, Copilot evidence, CFO Brief and Decision Ledger. Do not imply AI inference is live if the health / request result does not confirm it.

### Minutes 12–17: test willingness to pay
Ask:
- Would this solve a problem you have at least once a month?
- Which output would you act on?
- What would stop you trusting the result?
- What do you currently pay for finance support or tools?
- Would €59/month be reasonable for a reliable version? Why or why not?
- What would you need to see before connecting your real data?

Avoid asking only "Do you like it?" Interest is not willingness to pay.

### Minutes 17–20: close
Invite them to one of three next steps:
- a second session with their own synthetic or anonymized assumptions,
- a design-partner session once staging is safe for their data,
- a paid beta only after live AI, privacy, billing, data isolation and support are actually verified.

Never ask a prospect to upload confidential company financial records into a Preview that has not passed the security and privacy release gates.

## Objection handling

**"I can just use ChatGPT and a spreadsheet."**
That's a fair alternative. Avenor is valuable only if it saves time by keeping the company's assumptions and history in context, using repeatable financial calculations, showing evidence and saving decisions. Ask them to compare one real decision rather than debate AI in general.

**"I don't trust AI with company numbers."**
Explain the data boundary, access controls and retention policy truthfully. Calculations are deterministic and use supplied assumptions, while AI explains them. Do not claim security certifications or privacy guarantees that have not been independently verified.

**"We already have an accountant."**
Avenor is not trying to replace compliance or accounting advice. It is for the recurring operating decisions between monthly or quarterly accountant conversations.

**"€59/month is too much."**
Ask what they compare it with and what a wrong hiring/spending decision would cost. Do not discount immediately. If the value is not obvious, learn which job matters more.

## Prospect tracker

Track one row per founder with:
- Name / company / website / country / stage / business model
- Warm intro source
- Date contacted / follow-up date
- Discovery completed (yes/no)
- Current process and biggest pain
- Demo viewed / demo scenario tested
- Main objection / missing capability
- Willingness to pay at €59 (yes/no/unclear)
- Next step / owner / expected date
- Outcome (not a fit / interview / design partner / paid)

## Initial weekly targets

- 10 tailored first contacts
- 5 follow-ups
- 3 founder conversations
- 2 complete demos
- 1 explicit willingness-to-pay test

The first milestone is three genuine paid subscriptions: €177 gross MRR at €59/month, before VAT/tax, Stripe fees, refunds and costs. Free testers do not count as revenue.

## Product feedback loop

For every demo, write down:
1. the question the founder wanted answered,
2. the data they had available,
3. the data they could not provide,
4. the result they expected,
5. the result Avenor produced,
6. whether they trusted it,
7. what they would pay to repeat the workflow.

Use these observations to choose product work. Do not add features because they look impressive; add them when at least several target founders identify the same recurring problem.
